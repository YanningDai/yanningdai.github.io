PhD Student @ KAUST  |  Email = yanning.dai [at] kaust.edu.sa

Hi! I am a second-year PhD student working with Prof. Jürgen Schmidhuber at KAUST Center of Excellence for Generative AI. Previously, I received my M.S. and B.E. degrees in Electrical Engineering from Beihang University, advised by Prof. Shuo Gao in 2023 and 2020.

Research: My research interests span reinforcement learning, self-improving agents, and embodied AI. I study how agents can improve their capabilities through experience and feedback, with an emphasis on making this process efficient and reliable. I am particularly interested in how LLM-based agents refine their reasoning and problem-solving strategies, and how embodied agents connect reasoning with action to adapt to new tasks and environments.

Actively looking for research internship opportunities. Feel free to reach out for collaboration!
